Find the named country on your classroom map. Tap its numbered marker, then lock your guess. Choose a region or all Africa. Complete Map gives every player every selected worksheet location once; short matches use matching difficulty tiers.

Explorer 100 ptsNavigator 200 ptsExpert 300 pts

Tiers are classroom difficulty estimates, not rankings of importance. Smaller and harder-to-locate countries usually earn more. Correct timed guesses earn up to 20 extra points. Wrong guesses earn 0.

Rules and map coverage

Players take turns on this device. Other players should not help. One locked guess per turn. A regional hint halves the country points and removes the speed bonus. Each player has one optional boost: double the country points if correct; lose the country points if wrong (score never falls below zero). Speed points are not doubled.

The game uses the numbering and outlines from your supplied Seterra worksheet. The game covers all 52 numbered locations on your supplied worksheet. Western Sahara (#38) is a disputed territory and is included as a scored worksheet location. Eswatini replaces “Swaziland”; Côte d’Ivoire replaces “Ivory Coast.” The main map preserves your worksheet image and its leader lines. Regions follow the UN M49 scheme: Central means Middle Africa; Angola is Central, while Zambia, Zimbabwe, Malawi and Mozambique are East.
